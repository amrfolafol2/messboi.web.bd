Everything Built For Mess Life

Designed for Bachelor & Student Messes

Say goodbye to lost paper registers, messy notebooks, and disputed Excel sheets. Messboi makes your daily mess calculations instant and stress-free.

Flexible Meal Management

Choose 1, 2 or 3 meals per day and select Breakfast, Lunch or Dinner according to your mess meal structure.

Automatic Meal Tracking

Meals are automatically treated as ON by default unless manually turned OFF, saving you repetitive daily taps.

Expense Tracking

Track groceries (বাজার), outside food, snacks, transport, bills, mess manager expenses and other monthly costs.

Monthly Budget

Set a monthly mess deposit or personal budget and monitor your remaining funds and spending rate automatically.

Meal Rate Calculator

Set your dynamic or fixed meal rate and calculate exact individual food dues without math headaches.

Analytics & Statistics

View monthly meal counts, category-wise spending patterns, and balance breakdowns through clean visual charts.

Day-by-Day Calendar

See meal status and daily expense activity on an interactive calendar. Verify any specific date in seconds.

Offline First

Everything works 100% without internet. No loading spinners, server timeouts, or cellular data required.

Local Private Data

All personal records stay safe in your device storage. No tracking, no third-party ads, and no data harvesting.

Backup & Restore

Easily export your mess records into a safe local backup file and restore whenever switching phones.
